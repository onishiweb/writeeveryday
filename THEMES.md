# Prompt Themes Analysis

Analysis of all 96 prompts (January–April) grouped by theme, with gaps identified and future theme suggestions.

## Existing Themes

### 1. Single-word / Abstract Themes (~16 prompts)
Completion, laziness, loneliness, motivation, anxiety, adventure, chaos, warmth, companionship, exploration, quiet, rain, green, gold, grey, artificial, weight, silence, blue

> The backbone of the project. Consistent, memorable, and endlessly reinterpretable.

### 2. Genre Fiction Exercises (~15 prompts)
- **Fantasy**: discovering a power, world-building, magic going wrong, genie wish
- **Crime**: crime discovered, killer revealed
- **Sci-fi**: spaceship, opening scene, technology fails
- **Horror**: haunted house, uncanny neighbourhood, waking up somewhere unknown
- **Romance**: noticing a love interest, a romantic scene

> Well-distributed across genres. Currently light on literary fiction prompts that don't fit a clear genre.

### 3. Alternative Formats (~12 prompts)
Recipe, positive review, negative review, app review, screenplay dialogue, journal entry, letter to past self, complaint letter, email (the satirical one), comedic to-do list, a list that isn't a list, write something very short

> A real strength — these stretch writers into unfamiliar forms without requiring genre knowledge.

### 4. Emotional States & Psychology (~10 prompts)
Loneliness, anxiety, grief, motivation, laziness, waiting, exhaustion, procrastination, internal debate, making it through

> Heavy focus on anxiety-adjacent emotions. Missing joy, pride, jealousy, shame.

### 5. Relationships & Human Connection (~9 prompts)
Argument between friends, two people reconnecting, teaching, betrayal, companionship, an unwanted gift, knowing something you shouldn't, lied to all your life, a conversation where both people are right

> Good spread across conflict, tenderness, and complexity.

### 6. Craft & Technique Prompts (~8 prompts)
Scene with a twist, flashback scene, happy ending that feels wrong, scene told through objects, death of a hero/villain, write only using dialogue, write something loud, write something very short

> These are the most valuable for developing writers — they focus on *how* to write, not just *what*.

### 7. Sensory Prompts (~6 prompts)
A specific smell, something that doesn't taste good, the first taste of something extraordinary, physical exhaustion, warmth, something loud

> Under-represented relative to how useful sensory writing is as a skill.

### 8. Everyday Life & Mundane Settings (~6 prompts)
Dentist waiting room, breakfast, car salesman, interview prep, restaurant, email after a meeting

> Some of the best prompts here — the mundane is underrated in fiction and a useful challenge.

### 9. Character Work (~4 prompts)
Character backstory, villain's origin story, day in the life of a cat/dog

> Surprisingly thin for a writing practice site. Character is foundational.

### 10. Humour & Satire (~4 prompts)
Comedic to-do list, negative review, fantasy weapon sales pitch, email that didn't need to be sent

> A light but important vein. Comedy is technically demanding and rarely practiced.

### 11. Journey & Transition (~4 prompts)
Embarking on a journey, travelling together, celebrating something new, making it through

> Useful but could be broader — transitions are one of the richest areas of human experience.

---

## Gaps in the Current Prompts

- Very few prompts about **family** or intergenerational relationships
- No prompts focused on **place** as a primary subject
- No **mythology, folklore, or fairy tale** exercises
- No prompts about **childhood or memory** specifically
- No focus on **class, money, or social dynamics**
- Light on **nature and the physical world**
- Almost no **body-focused** writing (health, illness, physicality)
- No **moral dilemma** prompts beyond the genie wish

## Suggested Future Themes

Designed to complement what exists and fill the gaps above.

| Theme | Why it's valuable |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Belonging, displacement, safety — universally resonant |
| **Childhood** | Memory, innocence, the past as a foreign country |
| **Hunger** | Both literal and metaphorical — rich sensory and emotional territory |
| **Power** | Authority, rebellion, submission — cuts across every genre |
| **Nostalgia** | Distinct from memory — has a specific bittersweet quality worth exploring |
| **Shame** | Underexplored emotion, technically challenging to write well |
| **Pride** | Counterpoint to shame — covers ambition, arrogance, dignity |
| **Family** | Obligation, love, conflict, inheritance — an endless well |
| **Money** | Class, aspiration, desperation — rarely written about honestly |
| **Folklore / Myth** | Retelling, subverting, or updating ancient stories |
| **Weather / Seasons** | Beyond rain — snow, heat, the body in a season |
| **Illness & Recovery** | Vulnerability, dependency, the body as subject |
| **Place** | A street, a city, a landscape as the main character |
| **Faith & Doubt** | Belief, ritual, loss of faith — rich dramatic territory |
| **Jealousy** | One of fiction's great engines, rarely practiced directly |
| **Work** | Ambition, tedium, identity through labour |
| **Failure** | Specific, underwritten — distinct from grief or loss |
| **Obsession** | The fine line between passion and compulsion |
| **Silence** *(expanded)* | A whole month of silence variations could work as a dedicated theme |
| **The Body** | Physical sensation beyond exhaustion — desire, hunger, pain, rest |
| **Nature** | Animals, landscapes, seasons — mostly absent right now |
| **Technology** | AI, surveillance, devices — increasingly universal human experience |
| **Strangers** | Brief encounters, the lives of people we'll never know |
| **Endings** | Distinct from death scenes — farewells, last things, finishing |
| **Secrets** | One secret prompt exists — this theme has much more range |

## Monthly Theme Suggestion

Assigning one theme per month gives writers a sense of arc across the year and makes the prompt set feel curated rather than random. Example structure:

| Month | Theme |
|---|---|
| January | New Beginnings *(existing)* |
| February | Companionship / Love *(existing)* |
| March | Weight & Consequence *(existing)* |
| April | Power |
| May | Home |
| June | The Body |
| July | Strangers |
| August | Nature |
| September | Work & Ambition |
| October | Folklore & Myth |
| November | Family |
| December | Endings |
